During my internship at **Amazon Web Services Viet Nam Company Limited** from **May 19, 2026** to **August 17, 2026**, I joined the **Workforce Bootcamp - First Cloud AI Journey** program and developed the **EduFlow Platform**. This experience allowed me to apply software engineering knowledge to a practical product, covering requirements analysis, backend and frontend development, testing, CI/CD automation, and AWS deployment.

Through EduFlow, I strengthened my skills in **Spring Boot, Thymeleaf, Spring Security, JWT, OTP, VNPay, MySQL, Docker, Terraform, GitHub Actions, and AWS ECS Fargate**. I also developed practical experience in reading logs, resolving deployment issues, validating business workflows in a deployed environment, and producing technical documentation with Hugo.

In my working approach, I proactively monitored progress, verified the result of each significant change, and continued troubleshooting until the system became stable. Important changes were tested, recorded through commits or workflows, and verified again in the deployed environment.

## Objective

Enable administrators to operate accounts and instructors to manage their own course catalog.

## Work completed

- Built an administrator dashboard with system metrics and account lists.
- Added instructor creation, account status control, and form error feedback.
- Connected instructor courses, students, orders, and earnings pages to real APIs.
- Fixed broken routes and removed mock dashboard content.

## Outcome

Both operational roles have distinct, data-backed journeys. Instructors can only manage courses they own.

## Lesson learned

A useful dashboard answers concrete operational questions; decorative numbers disconnected from real data reduce trust.

## Objective

Understand the two Spring Boot applications and define the MVP scope for students, instructors, and administrators.

## Work completed

- Inventoried controllers, services, entities, templates, and frontend/backend configuration.
- Mapped registration, sign-in, catalog, checkout, and learning journeys.
- Identified static data, broken routes, and missing authorization checks.

## Outcome

The scope centered on three roles and the course lifecycle. The frontend on `:8080` and backend on `:8888` remained separate so they could be developed, tested, and deployed independently.

## Lesson learned

A journey-based inventory prioritizes defects that affect product value before infrastructure optimization begins.

This section introduces three technical blog posts about AWS and the EduFlow project. Their content comes from research, development, and system deployment work.

### [Blog 1 - Amazon RDS: High Availability, Backup, and Database Cost Optimization]({{% relref "3-BlogsPosted/3.1-Blog1" %}})

This post clarifies Multi-AZ, how it differs from Read Replicas, the backup and restore model, and four practical ways to optimize Amazon RDS costs.

### [Blog 2 - Reducing AWS Infrastructure Costs by up to 60% for Start-ups and Businesses]({{% relref "3-BlogsPosted/3.2-Blog2" %}})

This post presents three practical strategies: optimizing compute with AWS Graviton and Savings Plans, managing storage with S3 Lifecycle, and scheduling Dev/Test resources to stop outside working hours.

### [Blog 3 - EduFlow on AWS: Defense in Depth with VPC and ECS Fargate]({{% relref "3-BlogsPosted/3.3-Blog3" %}})
